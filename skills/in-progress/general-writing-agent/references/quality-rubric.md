## Rating bands

-   **90--100: Strong** --- ready after light proofreading, provided
    there are no critical failures.
-   **80--89: Good** --- publishable after focused revision.
-   **70--79: Needs revision** --- revise thesis, evidence, or structure
    before publication.
-   **Below 70: Rebuild** --- revisit the topic, material, or central
    argument.

## Critical failures (override the score)

A draft cannot be recommended for publication if it contains: -
fabricated personal experience or testimonials; - invented or materially
distorted data; - false quotation or attribution; - material
misrepresentation of a product or service; - plagiarism or close
imitation of a source author's distinctive expression; - a materially
misleading conclusion.

## Required review output

1.  Overall score and rating band.
2.  Dimension scores with concise reasons.
3.  Three strengths worth preserving.
4.  Up to three highest-priority problems.
5.  Specific revision actions, ordered by impact.
6.  Publish-readiness: ready / revise / rebuild.
7.  Explicitly identify any unverified claims.

## Revision order

1.  Factual accuracy and source fidelity.
2.  Thesis and argument.
3.  Evidence and examples.
4.  Structure and pacing.
5.  Opening and ending.
6.  Sentence-level clarity and rhythm.
7.  Formatting and proofreading.

Do not spend time polishing sentences while the thesis or evidence
remains broken.
